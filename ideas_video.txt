VIDEO IDEAS - Senus PLC Board Report
====================================

SUGGESTED LENGTH: 4-6 minutes, in English

1. HOOK (0:00-0:20)
   - The problem: a board report PDF is dozens of pages; finding the company's direction
     and the key numbers takes time and mistakes are easy.
   - The idea: upload the PDF and get a navigable board report where every figure is
     traced back to its source.

2. OVERVIEW / ARCHITECTURE (0:20-0:50)
   - Frontend: React + Vite + TypeScript.
   - Backend: FastAPI + SQLAlchemy + Alembic, PostgreSQL, all running with Docker Compose.
   - Pipeline: PDF -> extraction -> validation/reconciliation -> database -> API -> UI.
   - An LLM (Groq) is used only for the Company insights and the chat.

3. PDF INGESTION (0:50-1:40)
   - Upload the document and review what was extracted.
   - Extraction of P&L, balance sheet and cash flow, plus the narrative
     (clients, milestones, board and management).
   - Consistency checks: totals add up, cross-checks against notes, reconciliation between periods.
   - Key principle: anything without a source in the PDF is not shown.

4. COMPANY VIEW (1:40-3:10)  <- the highlight
   - Company direction: Board & Management, Customers & Partners, commercial pipeline,
     strategic milestones.
   - Objective chart: at least 50% CAGR on FY25 revenue (EUR 836,991) up to EUR 6.36M in FY30.
     Explain why there is NO forecast: a deliberate choice to show only verifiable data.
   - Three AI-generated insights (not quotes from the text) and consistency notes.
   - AI chat: ask something real, e.g. "What are the main risks around the balance sheet?" or
     "How does the reported growth compare with the target?"
     (Note: answers take ~30 s; trim the wait when editing.)

5. FINANCIALS VIEW (3:10-4:10)
   - KPIs, P&L, balance sheet and cash flow per period.
   - Compare periods: you only choose what to compare with; the selected period is the reference,
     and comparisons always run forward in time (past -> future).
   - Operating result waterfall.

6. QUALITY AND DECISIONS (4:10-4:50)
   - Tests: backend (pytest) and frontend (vitest), plus validation and reconciliation checks.
   - Cleanup: ~2,900 lines of unused code removed, AI removed from Financials.
   - Login and basic security.

7. WRAP-UP (4:50-5:20)
   - Lessons learned: verifiable data beats nice-looking estimates; AI is a helper, not a source.
   - Next steps: more reports and periods, deduplicate customers vs. milestones.
   - Call to action: repository / README.
   - One-line summary: "From the PDF to the board report, without making anything up."

RECORDING TIPS
--------------
- Prepare the session beforehand: log in (board@senus.com), data loaded, insights already generated (cached).
- Rehearse the chat question to avoid dead time; cut the wait in editing.
- Browser zoom at 110-125% so text is readable.
- Show Company first (the story), then Financials (the evidence).
- Never show the .env file or API keys on screen.

POINTS TO MENTION HONESTLY
--------------------------
- The objective chart is arithmetic, not a prediction.
- "Total creditors" (EUR 463.6k) is the sum of short- and long-term creditors; the report's
  "bank debt" of EUR 76.5k corresponds to creditors due after more than one year only.
- First Milk / Biochar appear both under customers and under milestones.
